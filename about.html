About FO3S

Oman's first student-led free and open source software society — founded at Sultan Qaboos University in 2010 to build an open-source culture from the ground up.

// رسالتنا — our mission

Why we exist

FO3S was founded on a simple belief: technology is most powerful when it's free and open. At Sultan Qaboos University, we saw a community of curious, driven students with no structured pathway into the world of open-source software. We built one.

Our mission is to spread free and open source software culture at SQU and across Oman — through education, collaboration, and hands-on building. We run workshops, produce resources, support members with labs and mentorship, and contribute to real-world FOSS projects.

We are not a coding club. We are a movement — aligned with the global FOSS philosophy that knowledge, software, and innovation must be freely shared. From our founding partnership with the Information Technology Authority (ITA), to our long-term goal of building Oman's first GNU/Linux distribution, everything we do is in service of that mission.

// الشراكة الوطنية — national partnership

Backed by the Information Technology Authority

FO3S operates under a formal memorandum of understanding with Oman's ITA — part of the national Digital Strategy to develop the ICT sector through open-source adoption.

🏛️

National FOSS Support Initiative — مبادرة دعم البرمجيات الحرة والمفتوحة المصدر

Launched in March 2010 during the Free Software Seminar in Muscat, the initiative coordinates governmental institutions, professional bodies, and universities to build a sustainable FOSS ecosystem in the Sultanate of Oman. FO3S at SQU is the university arm of this effort.

// رؤيتنا — our vision

Where we're headed

Four long-term goals that shape everything FO3S does.

01

An open-source SQU

Transitioning SQU's student-facing tools and infrastructure to free and open source alternatives — starting with what we can build ourselves.

02

Oman's first GNU/Linux distro

Shipping an Arabic-first, Omani-made Linux distribution that any institution can adopt — reducing proprietary software dependency at the national level.

03

A generation of contributors

Equipping SQU graduates with the mindset and skills to contribute to global FOSS projects — and carry the culture into their careers and organisations.

04

Arabic FOSS ecosystem

Supporting and producing open-source tools, documentation, and resources that serve Arabic-speaking users — a currently underserved community in FOSS.

// المشروع الرئيسي — flagship project

The Omani GNU/Linux Distribution

One of FO3S's most ambitious long-term goals is building Oman's first homegrown GNU/Linux distribution — an Arabic-first, locally-maintained operating system that can be adopted by schools, universities, and government institutions.

  • Replace proprietary operating systems in Omani educational institutions
  • Provide a stable, Arabic-language Linux experience out of the box
  • Build and maintain it entirely through student and community contributions
  • Release under a free software license so anyone can use, modify, and redistribute it
