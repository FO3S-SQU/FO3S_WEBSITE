// من نحن — who we are

FO3S – Free & Open Source Software Society

Founded in 2010 under the supervision of the Communication and Information Research Center at SQU, following a memorandum of understanding with the Information Technology Authority (ITA). We believe openness, collaboration, and free knowledge are the foundation of meaningful technology.

🎙️

Conferences, exhibitions & workshops

Regular technical events open to all students — from install fests to full-day symposiums on open-source technology and culture.

📰

Projects, newsletters & printed materials

We produce resources that spread FOSS knowledge across the university and beyond — both digital and in print.

🧪

Community labs & technical support

Access our equipped labs, get hands-on help, and find mentorship from peers who've been building in the open-source world.

🇴🇲

The first Omani GNU/Linux distribution

Our flagship long-term project — building an Omani-made GNU/Linux distro and replacing proprietary software in institutions with free alternatives.

// ما هي البرمجيات الحرة — what is FOSS

The four essential freedoms

Software is considered "free" when its users have these four freedoms. Governments across Brazil, Germany, Russia, Belgium, Venezuela and others have adopted FOSS as policy.

00

Freedom to run

Run the program for any purpose, without restriction or limitation.

حرية تشغيل البرنامج لأي غرض كان
01

Freedom to study

Study how the program works and adapt it to your needs. Access to source code is required.

حرية دراسة كيفية عمل البرنامج وتعديله
02

Freedom to redistribute

Share copies freely so you can help your community and the wider world.

حرية توزيع نسخ من إصداراتك المعدلة
03

Freedom to improve

Improve the program and release your improvements publicly so the whole community benefits.

حرية تطوير البرنامج وتحسينه وإظهارها للعالم
// المبادرة الوطنية — national initiative

Oman's FOSS support initiative

Part of Oman's Digital Strategy for developing the ICT sector, launched March 2010 during the Free Software Seminar in Muscat.

🇴🇲

National FOSS Support Initiative — مبادرة دعم البرمجيات الحرة

Supported by the Information Technology Authority (ITA) in collaboration with governmental and professional institutions across Oman, the initiative aims to build a sustainable open-source ecosystem in the Sultanate.

  • Promote FOSS as a viable alternative to proprietary software across institutions
  • Build human capacity in FOSS through specialized training programs
  • Encourage research, development, and innovation in the FOSS ecosystem
