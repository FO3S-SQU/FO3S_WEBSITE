01 — The whole

One mark, held together

From a distance the FO3S logo looks like one solid shape. Look closer and it is a key, a gear, and three open hands — parts that only mean something when they hold together.

02 — The break

Take it apart

Free software means you are allowed to break it open. Read every line, question every choice, keep what works and rebuild the rest — no permission required.

03 — Four freedoms

Run. Study. Share. Improve.

Use it for any purpose. See exactly how it works. Pass copies to a friend. Publish your improvements so the next person starts further ahead than you did.
